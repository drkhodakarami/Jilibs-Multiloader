# Potestas

**"Potestas"** (pronounced *poh-TEH-stas*) — Latin for "power, capability, authority — the mastery over force itself."

In the Ars Mundi ecosystem, Potestas is the beating heart of every industrial system. Without energy, machines are just expensive decoration.

## What's Inside

**Six Generators:**
- **Solarium** — Converts sunlight into energy. Simple, reliable, always working during the day.
- **Thermum** — Harvests heat from (internal tank) or fire beneath Where there's fire, there's power.
- **Motus** — Kinetic energy from water movement and rotation.
- **Ventus** — Wind power. Place it high and watch it work.
- **Infernum** — Born from the Nether itself. Extreme heat, extreme output.
- **Resonator** — End-energy resonance. The pinnacle of generation.

**Energy Storage (Reservoirs):**
Storage blocks built from every material tier — Wood through Exodium. Each material determines capacity. Four gem-tier options give you choice, not just progression.

**Established Integration:**
Potestas plugs into existing Minecraft energy infrastructure. No new API. No proprietary system. It just works.

## How It Fits

Potestas depends on **Materia** (especially Energetic Alloy). It belongs to **Ars Mundi** only — not Ars Vitae. It powers **Machinum** machines and feeds **Fistula** transport networks.

## Why You Want It

Solarium on your roof. Thermum beside your lava farm. Resonator in your End base. Potestas gives you the energy foundation to power everything else — and six different ways to generate it.
