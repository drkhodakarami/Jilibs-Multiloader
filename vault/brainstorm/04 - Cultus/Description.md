# Cultus

**"Cultus"** (pronounced *KUL-tus*) — Latin for "cultivation, refinement, adornment — the art of making something beautiful."

In a world of machines and power, Cultus reminds you that Minecraft is also about creation and beauty.

## What's Inside

**Complete Architectural Sets per Material:**
For every gem and exotic material, Cultus provides a full building vocabulary:
- Full Blocks, Slabs, Stairs
- Doors, Trapdoors, Buttons
- Walls, Fences, Fence Gates

**Materials with Visual Identity:**
- **Carminis** blocks — Warm, rich tones. Royalty and fire.
- **Glaucus** blocks — Cool, elegant blues. Aquatic grace.
- **Flavum** blocks — Golden brilliance. Prosperity and light.
- **Exodium** blocks — Dark, mysterious textures. The End brought to your build.

**Pure Creative Expression:**
No machines. No automation. No processing chains. Just beautiful blocks waiting for your vision.

## How It Fits

Cultus depends on **Materia** for its materials. It belongs to both **Ars Vitae** and **Ars Mundi**. It's the mod that lets your rare gems and materials shine in your builds — literally.

## Why You Want It

You mined that Carminis. You earned that Exodium. Now build with it. Cultus turns rare materials into architectural masterpieces, giving every gem block the visual impact it deserves.
