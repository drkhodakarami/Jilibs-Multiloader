# Materia

**"Materia"** (pronounced *meh-TEH-ree-ah*) — Latin for "element, fundamental substance, the matter from which all things are made."

This name is exactly what this mod is: the foundation that every other part of the Ars Mundi ecosystem is built upon. Before there are tools, armor, machines, or energy — there must first be *matter*.

## What's Inside

Materia introduces a rich material vocabulary to Minecraft:

**Gems with Identity:**
- **Carminis** (*KAR-mi-nis*) — A deep red gemstone representing vitality, heat, and raw intensity. Think ruby, but with purpose.
- **Glaucus** (*GLAW-kus*) — A clear blue gemstone evoking sky and ocean clarity. Think sapphire, but connected to depth and control.
- **Flavum** (*FLA-vum*) — A brilliant golden gemstone radiating brightness and disruption. Think citrine, but alive with energy.
- **Exodium** (*ek-SO-dee-um*) — An exotic material from the End itself. Not just a stronger metal — it represents the unknown.

**Functional Alloys:**
- **Signalum** — Conducts signals, powers redstone systems.
- **Resistant Alloy** — Built for containment and extreme durability.
- **Vibrant Alloy** — Alive with movement and dynamic energy.
- **Energetic Alloy** — The bridge between matter and energy.
- **Exodium Alloy** — Exotic properties meet material science.
- **Thermium** — Pure thermal behavior, forged for heat.

**Universal Components:**
Gears, Rods, Plates, Reinforced Plates, Dust, and Nuggets — the shared language every Ars Mundi mod speaks.

## How It Fits

Materia is **required** by every single mod in the ecosystem. Whether you install just the survival-focused **Ars Vitae** pack or the full industrial **Ars Mundi** experience, Materia is always the first piece.

## Why You Want It

Every great build starts with understanding what your materials can do. Materia gives Minecraft's material system the depth it always deserved — and every other mod in this ecosystem builds on top of it.
