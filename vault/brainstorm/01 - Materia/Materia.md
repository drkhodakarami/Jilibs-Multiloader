# Materia - Mod Architecture Knowledge Base

## Overview

Materia is the foundational material mod of the [[Ars Mundi]] ecosystem.

Name:

Materia

Pronunciation:

mater-yah

Meaning:

Materia is derived from the Latin concept of:

- element
- fundamental substance
- basic matter
- building block

The name represents the idea that everything in the ecosystem begins with materials.

Before there are:

- tools
- armor
- machines
- energy systems
- transport networks
- advanced abilities

there must first be matter.

Materia is the foundation upon which every other Ars Mundi module is built.

Rules: 

- An item being registered by Materia does not imply that Materia must provide a recipe for that item.
- Creative tabs represent ownership and conceptual classification, not progression availability.


---

# Role in Ars Mundi

Ars Mundi is a modular Minecraft ecosystem built around different disciplines of world mastery.

Each mod represents a specific field.

Materia represents:

> The understanding and organization of the materials that make up the world.

It is the material science foundation of the ecosystem.

Other mods answer questions such as:

- How can materials be shaped?
- How can materials be processed?
- How can materials empower the player?
- How can materials alter the world?

Materia only answers:

> What materials exist?

---

# Core Philosophy

## Materia Is Not a Technology Mod

Materia should never become a technology mod.

It does not contain:

- machines
- processors
- factories
- automation
- generators
- cables
- industrial systems

Those belong to other Ars Mundi modules.

Materia defines the raw vocabulary.

Other modules define the applications.

---

## Materia Is Not a Progression Ladder

Materia materials are not designed as simple numerical upgrades.

The goal is not:

iron → stronger iron → stronger material → strongest material

Instead, every material should have an identity.

A material exists because it represents a property, behavior, or concept.

Examples:

- Signalum represents communication and signals.
- Energetic Alloy represents energy interaction.
- Resistant Alloy represents containment and durability.
- Exodium represents exotic End-related properties.

---

# Why Materia Exists

Minecraft already contains many fundamental materials:

- iron
- gold
- copper
- diamonds
- netherite

However, a large ecosystem requires a shared material language.

Without Materia, every mod would create its own:

- metals
- components
- crafting ingredients
- naming conventions

This causes fragmentation.

Materia solves this by becoming the common material foundation.

Every Ars Mundi mod depends on Materia because every system needs consistent materials.

---

# Design Rules

## Rule 1: Define Materials, Not Usage

Materia defines what a material is.

It does not define how the material is used.

Example:

Materia:

A gear exists.

Machinum:

A gear is manufactured using a machine.

Arma:

A gear is used in a tool recipe.

Lorica:

A material is used in armor.

---

## Rule 2: Shared Vocabulary

All ecosystem mods should use Materia materials instead of creating duplicate versions.

Example:

A machine mod should not create:

- machine gear
- industrial gear
- advanced gear

It should use:

Materia Gear

The production method may differ, but the material identity remains consistent.

---

## Rule 3: Avoid Unnecessary Complexity

Materia should provide only universal concepts.

It should not add:

- specialized circuits
- machine parts
- magical artifacts
- advanced automation components

Those belong to specialized modules.

# Exotic Material

## Exodium

Meaning:

Exodium is an exotic material strongly associated with:

- the End dimension
- Endermen
- unusual physics
- strange properties

The name was chosen instead of "Enderite".

Reason:

Enderite creates the impression of:

- a direct Netherite replacement
- only a stronger tier
- a simple progression upgrade

Exodium represents something different.

It is not just stronger.

It represents unknown properties from the End.

Because the ecosystem heavily uses End-related concepts, Exodium acts as a bridge between normal material science and exotic behavior.

# Gems

Materia contains three primary gems.

The purpose of these gems is to create unique identities rather than using only common gemstone names.

---

# Carminis

Represents:

Ruby

Concept:

- red gemstone
- intensity
- vitality
- strength

The name provides a unique identity while preserving the association with ruby.

---

# Glaucus

Represents:

Sapphire

Pronunciation:

GLAW-kus

Concept:

- blue gemstone
- clarity
- deep blue coloration
- connection to sky and ocean tones

The name avoids conflicts with existing Minecraft concepts such as lapis.

---

# Flavum

Represents:

Citrine

Concept:

- yellow gemstone
- brightness
- golden coloration

The name creates a unified gemstone identity while avoiding the generic use of "citrine".

---

# Alloys

Alloys in Materia represent properties.

They are not traditional technology tiers.

Each alloy exists because it represents a specific behavior.

---

# Signalum

Concept:

Signal and communication.

Purpose:

Signalum represents materials related to:

- information transfer
- redstone systems
- control systems

Primary future usage:

Impulsus and other signal-related systems.

---

# Resistant Alloy

Concept:

Resistance and containment.

Purpose:

A material designed for systems that must withstand external forces.

Potential usage:

- fluid systems
- gas systems
- pressure systems
- protective structures

The focus is not simply hardness.

The focus is stability.

---

# Vibrant Alloy

Concept:

Activity and movement.

Purpose:

A material associated with dynamic systems.

Potential usage:

- item transport
- movement systems
- active mechanisms

The concept is motion.

---

# Energetic Alloy

Concept:

Energy interaction.

Purpose:

A material associated with energy systems.

Primary usage:

Potestas.

Energetic Alloy belongs in Materia because the material itself is universal.

However:

- generators
- storage blocks
- energy systems

belong to Potestas.

---

# Exodium Alloy

Concept:

Exotic material behavior.

Purpose:

A combination of normal material science and End-related properties.

Potential usage:

- advanced systems
- exotic technology
- special ecosystem mechanics

---

# Thermium

Concept:

Heat and thermal behavior.

Purpose:

A material associated with:

- heat resistance
- thermal systems
- temperature interaction

---

# Components

Components are universal material forms.

They describe what a material has become.

They are intentionally generic.

---

# Gear

Meaning:

A mechanical component.

Represents:

- rotation
- mechanical interaction
- moving systems

A gear is not a machine item.

It is a material form.

The machine that creates it belongs to Machinum.

---

# Rod

Meaning:

A structural material form.

Represents:

- shafts
- supports
- long components

Used broadly throughout the ecosystem.

---

# Plate

Meaning:

A flattened material form.

Represents:

- sheets
- panels
- structural surfaces

Used by many future systems.

---

# Reinforced Plate

Meaning:

A stronger structural plate.

Represents:

- layered construction
- improved durability
- advanced material strength

---

# Dust

Meaning:

A fine material state.

Represents:

- powdered materials
- processed resources
- intermediate states

---

# Nugget

Meaning:

A small material subdivision.

Represents:

- partial material quantities
- flexible crafting amounts

---

# Recipe Philosophy

Materia defines item identity.

It does not define the final production philosophy.

The same component may have different acquisition methods depending on the installed ecosystem.

Example:

A Gear exists in Materia.

Without Machinum:

The Gear may be created through a crafting recipe.

With Machinum:

The Gear may require machine processing.

The item remains the same.

Only the production path changes.
