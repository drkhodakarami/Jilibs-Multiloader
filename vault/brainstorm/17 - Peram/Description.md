# Peram

**"Peram"** (pronounced *PEH-ram*) — Latin for "bag, pouch, traveling satchel — storage that goes where you go."

Your base has Cista. But you're not always at your base. Peram puts your storage on your back.

## What's Inside

**Normal Backpack:**
- Extra inventory slots you carry with you
- Craft from Materia materials
- Essential for exploration, mining, and long trips

**Ender-Linked Backpack:**
- Connected to the same storage network as Ender Chests
- Access your remote storage from anywhere
- The ultimate mobile inventory — your base's resources at your fingertips

**What Peram Means for Gameplay:**
- Explore caves without leaving loot behind
- Mine longer without returning to dump inventory
- Travel further with everything you need
- Ender-Linked = bridge between portable and base storage

## How It Fits

Peram depends on **Materia**. It belongs to both **Ars Vitae** and **Ars Mundi**. It complements **Cista** (stationary) — together they cover all storage needs. The Ender-Linked variant connects to vanilla Ender Chest mechanics.

## Why You Want It

The Ender-Linked backpack alone is worth the install. Full access to your base ender storage while exploring 10,000 blocks away. No more "inventory full, guess I'm going home" moments.
