# Peram - Mod Architecture Knowledge Base

## Overview

Peram is the portable storage mod of the [Ars Mundi](https://chatgpt.com/c/Ars%20Mundi) ecosystem.

Name:

Peram

Pronunciation:

PEH-ram

Meaning:

Peram comes from Latin and refers to:

- bag
    
- pouch
    
- traveling bag
    
- satchel
    
- container carried by a person
    

The name represents the purpose of this mod:

> Providing storage that travels with the player.

Peram focuses specifically on **portable storage**.

It provides:

- backpacks
    
- expanded personal storage
    
- portable inventory systems
    

Peram answers:

> How can the player carry more of their world with them?

---

# Role in Ars Mundi

Ars Mundi is divided into different disciplines.

Each mod represents a specific area of mastery.

Peram represents:

> The discipline of mobility, carrying, and personal storage.

[Cista](https://chatgpt.com/c/Cista) provides:

- stationary storage
    
- chests
    
- tanks
    

Peram provides:

- backpacks
    
- portable storage
    

Peram answers:

> How can storage remain available while the player is away from their base?

---

# Core Philosophy

## Storage Should Travel With The Player

Minecraft already provides several forms of stationary storage.

A chest stays where it is placed.

A barrel stays where it is placed.

A storage system built into a base cannot follow the player into the world.

Peram expands storage into the player's personal equipment.

The purpose is simple:

> The player should be able to carry a meaningful amount of storage without constantly returning to base.

---

# Peram Is Not Cista

A critical design separation exists between Peram and [Cista](https://chatgpt.com/c/Cista).

Cista provides:

- stationary storage
    
- chests
    
- tanks
    
- large storage infrastructure
    

Peram provides:

- portable storage
    
- backpacks
    
- personal inventory expansion
    

The distinction is:

> Cista stores things in the world.

> Peram stores things with the player.

This keeps stationary and portable storage as separate systems.

---

# Backpack Philosophy

Backpacks are the primary content of Peram.

A backpack is intended to function as an extension of the player's personal inventory.

The player should be able to:

- carry additional items
    
- access stored items while traveling
    
- organize resources away from their base
    
- bring additional supplies on long journeys
    

The backpack should feel like a natural extension of the player's equipment rather than another chest simply placed inside the player's inventory.

---

# Portable Storage

The defining characteristic of Peram is portability.

A Peram backpack can travel with the player through:

- caves
    
- oceans
    
- the Nether
    
- the End
    
- other dimensions
    
- long exploration journeys
    

This makes Peram particularly useful when the player is far away from their main storage infrastructure.

---

# Backpack Progression

Peram can provide backpacks with different tiers.

The progression should make use of the established materials of Ars Mundi wherever appropriate.

Potential materials include:

- leather
    
- wood
    
- stone
    
- iron
    
- copper
    
- gold
    
- Carminis
    
- Glaucus
    
- Flavum
    
- amethyst
    
- diamond
    
- emerald
    
- Exodium
    

The exact progression and capacity of each backpack should be defined independently from the core architecture.

The important principle is:

> Better materials allow more capable portable storage.

---

# Material Identity

Advanced backpacks should retain the identity of their materials.

A Carminis backpack should communicate Carminis.

A Glaucus backpack should communicate Glaucus.

A Flavum backpack should communicate Flavum.

An Exodium backpack should communicate its exotic nature.

The storage system therefore becomes another way for the player to visually express their progression.

---

# Exodium Backpack

Exodium represents one of the highest levels of material progression in Ars Mundi.

An Exodium backpack can therefore represent the pinnacle of portable storage.

It can provide:

- extremely high capacity
    
- advanced organization
    
- late-game utility
    
- exotic storage capabilities
    

The purpose is not simply to create the largest backpack.

It should represent the culmination of the portable-storage progression.

---

# Backpack Accessibility

Portable storage should remain convenient.

The player should be able to access the backpack without having to return to a base.

This is the primary advantage of Peram over Cista.

However, convenience should not completely remove the value of stationary storage.

Large-scale resource management should still favor Cista.

Peram is for:

> What the player needs to carry.

Cista is for:

> What the player needs to keep.

---

# Backpack Organization

A backpack should help the player organize the resources carried during exploration.

It can be used for:

- mining resources
    
- building materials
    
- food
    
- tools
    
- equipment
    
- adventure supplies
    
- collected items
    

Different backpacks or backpack tiers may eventually provide specialized organization features.

However, the fundamental purpose remains portable inventory expansion.

---

# Relationship With Cista

Cista and Peram represent two sides of storage.

Cista:

> Stationary storage.

Peram:

> Portable storage.

A player may use both simultaneously.

For example:

The player stores most resources in a Cista chest.

↓

The player prepares for an expedition.

↓

Useful resources are placed into a Peram backpack.

↓

The player leaves the base.

↓

The backpack remains available throughout the journey.

This creates a natural relationship between the two storage systems.

---

# Relationship With Vas

[Vas](https://chatgpt.com/c/Vas) provides containment for:

- fluids
    
- gases
    
- other non-solid materials
    

Peram primarily focuses on portable item storage.

The distinction is:

> Vas defines vessels for non-solid materials.

> Peram provides portable storage for the player's carried resources.

If portable containment for fluids or gases is introduced in the future, it should be evaluated carefully to determine whether it belongs in Peram or should use Vas's containment systems.

---

# Relationship With Materia

[Materia](https://chatgpt.com/c/Materia) provides the materials used throughout Ars Mundi.

Peram uses those materials to create progressively more advanced backpacks.

The same materials can therefore participate in multiple forms of progression.

For example:

Materia:

Exodium exists.

Peram:

Exodium backpack exists.

The material becomes another expression of player progression.

---

# Relationship With Arma

[Arma](https://chatgpt.com/c/Arma) provides tools.

Peram allows the player to carry those tools and the resources needed to use them.

A player preparing for a long expedition may use a Peram backpack to carry:

- mining tools
    
- building blocks
    
- food
    
- materials
    
- utility items
    

Arma determines what the player can do.

Peram determines how much of the required equipment the player can carry.

---

# Relationship With Lorica

[Lorica](https://chatgpt.com/c/Lorica) provides armor and personal protection.

Peram provides personal storage.

Together they form part of the player's equipment system.

The distinction remains:

> Lorica protects the player.

> Peram carries the player's supplies.

---

# Relationship With Praxis

[Praxis](https://chatgpt.com/c/Praxis) provides player-oriented utility and abilities.

Peram complements these abilities by allowing the player to carry the items needed to use them.

For example, a player traveling between dimensions with Iterum can carry additional supplies through their Peram backpack.

The two systems therefore complement mobility:

> Praxis expands where the player can go.

> Peram expands what the player can carry.

---

# Relationship With Augmentum

[Augmentum](https://chatgpt.com/c/Augmentum) provides upgrade systems throughout Ars Mundi.

Peram backpacks may potentially accept compatible augmentations.

Possible upgrades could improve:

- capacity
    
- organization
    
- accessibility
    
- specialized storage behavior
    

Any such system should remain optional and should not make every backpack dependent on Augmentum.

The distinction remains:

> Peram provides the backpack.

> Augmentum improves compatible systems.

---

# Portable Storage and Progression

Peram should not completely replace the need for a base.

Portable storage is intentionally limited compared with the total amount of storage a developed base can provide.

The player should still have reasons to build:

- storage rooms
    
- warehouses
    
- workshops
    
- machine areas
    
- large Cista storage systems
    

Peram solves the problem of **carrying**.

Cista solves the problem of **keeping**.

# Ars Mundi Relationship

In the complete Ars Mundi ecosystem, Peram provides the **portable storage layer**.

Cista stores resources at the player's base.

Peram stores resources while the player is away.

Together they create a complete storage philosophy:

> **Cista for what stays.**

> **Peram for what travels.**

---

# Long-Term Philosophy

Peram should remain focused on portability.

Whenever a new storage feature is proposed, the question should be:

> Is this fundamentally about carrying storage with the player?

If yes:

It may belong in Peram.

If it instead focuses on:

- stationary storage
    
- fluid containment
    
- extraction
    
- processing
    
- transportation
    
- energy
    

it should belong to another mod.

This keeps Peram from becoming a generic storage mod.

---

# Final Philosophy

Peram represents the player's ability to carry their resources beyond the boundaries of their base.

The world is large.

Exploration takes time.

Resources are found far away.

The player should not always have to turn around simply because their inventory is full.

Cista provides the warehouse.

Peram provides the bag.

The player carries their supplies into the world and keeps moving forward.

**Peram — Storage That Travels.**
