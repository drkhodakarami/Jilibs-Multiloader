# Altum - Mod Architecture Knowledge Base

## Overview

Altum is the vertical space, void, and sky utility mod of the [[Ars Mundi]] ecosystem.

Name:

Altum

Pronunciation:

AL-tum

Meaning:

Altum comes from the Latin concept of:

- height
- depth
- the high places
- the upper world
- the deep or elevated space

The name represents the purpose of this mod:

> Providing control over vertical space and places where normal blocks cannot exist.

---

# Role in Ars Mundi

Ars Mundi is divided into different disciplines.

Each mod represents a specific area of mastery.

Altum represents:

> The discipline of vertical space and unreachable environments.

While other mods focus on:

- materials
- tools
- machines
- player abilities
- storage
- transportation
- world transformation

Altum focuses on places where normal building rules do not apply.

---

# Core Philosophy

## Mastering The Empty Space

Minecraft worlds are built around solid terrain.

Players naturally interact with:

- ground
- walls
- structures
- landscapes

However, the world also contains:

- sky
- void
- unreachable areas
- empty dimensions

Altum exists to give players control over these spaces.

The philosophy:

> Empty space is not a limitation. It is another place to build.

---

# Altum Is Not A Transportation Mod

Although Altum contains elevators, it is not a general transportation system.

It does not contain:

- pipes
- item transport
- fluid transport
- logistics systems

Transportation infrastructure belongs to:

- [[Fistula]]

Altum only handles movement related to vertical space.

---

# Altum Is Not A Building Mod

Although Altum allows building in impossible locations, it does not add decorative or structural blocks.

Decoration belongs to:

- [[Cultus]]

Storage belongs to:

- [[Cista]]

Construction materials belong to:

- [[Materia]]

Altum provides the ability to exist and build in unusual locations.

---

# Altum Blocks

Altum contains blocks related to:

- vertical movement
- sky construction
- void placement
- unreachable environments

---

# Elevator

## Purpose

The Altum Elevator provides vertical transportation between aligned elevator blocks.

---

## Functionality

When a player activates an elevator:

The player is teleported vertically to another elevator block aligned above or below.

The system searches for compatible elevator instances along the vertical axis.

---

## Design Philosophy

The elevator avoids traditional movement limitations.

Instead of:

- climbing towers
- building long staircases
- using external transport systems

the player can directly move through vertical space.

---

## Possible Uses

Examples:

- tall bases
- sky platforms
- underground facilities
- vertical farms
- large structures

---

# Angel Block

## Purpose

The Angel Block allows placement in empty space.

---

## Functionality

Normally in Minecraft:

Blocks require another block surface to be placed against.

Angel Block removes this limitation.

The player can place it:

- in the void
- floating in the sky
- without nearby supporting blocks

---

## Design Philosophy

Angel Block represents the first connection between the player and empty space.

It allows the player to begin construction where normal rules prevent building.

# Ars Vitae Relationship

Altum strongly supports the [[Ars Vitae]] philosophy.

It focuses on improving player freedom without forcing technological progression.

A player using only:

- Materia
- Arma
- Lorica
- Praxis
- Altum

can create unique structures without needing:

- machines
- energy systems
- automation
