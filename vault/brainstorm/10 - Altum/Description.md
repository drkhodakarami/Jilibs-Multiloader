# Altum

**"Altum"** (pronounced *AL-tum*) — Latin for "height, depth, the high places — mastering the spaces between earth and sky."

Minecraft's world has ground, walls, and ceilings. Altum conquers the empty space between them.

## What's Inside

**Elevator Block:**
- Place multiple elevator blocks vertically aligned
- Travel between them instantly
- Works across any distance within range
- Enables massive vertical structures

**Angel Block:**
- Place it in mid-air — no support surface needed
- Build floating platforms, sky bridges, structures in the void
- Breaks Minecraft's placement rules for blocks above empty space

**What This Means:**
- Build floating skybases with no scaffolding
- Create vertical transport in massive towers
- Reach impossible locations without bridging
- Enable Cultus decoration in places vanilla won't allow

## How It Fits

Altum depends on **Materia**. It belongs to both **Ars Vitae** and **Ars Mundi**. It's pure spatial mastery — no energy, no complex systems, just control over vertical space.

## Why You Want It

You've always wanted to build that floating island. That tower with instant travel between floors. That sky bridge over a ravine. Altum makes the impossible places buildable.
