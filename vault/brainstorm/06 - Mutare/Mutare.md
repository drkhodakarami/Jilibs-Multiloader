# Mutare - Mod Architecture Knowledge Base

## Overview

Mutare is the world manipulation and environmental transformation mod of the [[Ars Mundi]] ecosystem.

Name:

Mutare

Pronunciation:

mu-TA-re

Meaning:

Mutare comes from the Latin concept of:

- change
- transformation
- alteration
- replacement
- shifting from one state into another

The name represents the purpose of this mod:

> Changing the world itself according to the player's intent.

---

# Role in Ars Mundi

Ars Mundi is divided into different disciplines.

Each mod represents a specific area of mastery.

Mutare represents:

> The discipline of environmental transformation.

While other mods focus on:

- materials
- tools
- machines
- player abilities
- storage
- transportation

Mutare focuses on changing the environment itself.

---

# Core Philosophy

## Transformation Through Potential

Mutare is not a traditional technology system.

It does not represent factories, machines, or industrial progression.

Instead, it represents the point where the player gains enough mastery to directly influence the world.

The philosophy:

> The player does not only survive in the world. The player reshapes it.

---

# Mutare And Virtus Relationship

Mutare is directly connected to [[Virtus]].

The Mutare blocks are not created through normal crafting.

Instead, the player creates a dormant transformation material:

## Mutatio

Pronunciation:

mu-TA-ti-o

Meaning:

- change
- transformation
- alteration

Mutatio represents unrealized transformation potential.

---

# Mutatio

## Purpose

Mutatio is the intermediate item used to create all Mutare transformation blocks.

It does not have a final purpose by itself.

It represents a state before transformation.

---

## Creation

The exact recipe is not finalized.

The intended recipe uses transformation-related materials.

Possible ingredients:

- wool
- Exodium ore

or:

- wool
- Ender Pearl

The purpose is to connect Mutare with:

- transformation
- rare materials
- Virtus activation

---

# Virtus Activation

Using Virtus, the player activates Mutatio.

The process:

```
Mutatio

↓

Virtus Activation

↓

Specific Mutare Block
```


The same intermediate item can become different transformation abilities depending on the activated form.

---

# Philosophy Of Mutatio

Mutatio represents potential before completion.

Before activation:

It is only possibility.

After activation:

It becomes a specific transformation.

This follows the philosophy of Virtus:

> Potential exists everywhere. Virtus reveals it.

---

# Mutare Blocks

Mutare contains specialized transformation blocks.

Each block performs one focused environmental change.

---

# Fontis

## Meaning

Fontis means:

- spring
- fountain
- source of water

## Purpose

Fontis is the water generation Mutare block.

---

## Functionality

When activated:

Fontis generates water blocks within a predefined distance.

The generated water behaves as normal Minecraft water.

---

# Siccus

## Meaning

Siccus means:

- dry
- without moisture

## Purpose

Siccus is the water removal Mutare block.

---

## Functionality

When activated:

Siccus removes water blocks within a predefined distance.

The removed water blocks become air.

---

# Calor

## Meaning

Calor means:

- heat
- warmth
- thermal energy

## Purpose

Calor is the lava generation Mutare block.

---

## Functionality

When activated:

Calor generates lava blocks within a predefined distance.

The generated lava behaves as normal Minecraft lava.

---

# Siccatio

## Meaning

Siccatio means:

- drying
- removal of moisture

## Purpose

Siccatio is the lava removal Mutare block.

---

## Functionality

When activated:

Siccatio removes lava blocks within a predefined distance.

The removed lava blocks become air.

---

# Fodina

## Meaning

Fodina means:

- mine
- excavation
- underground digging

## Purpose

Fodina is the tunneling Mutare block.

---

## Functionality

When activated:

Fodina:

- digs a tunnel forward
- breaks blocks in its path
- collects block drops
- places all drops at the player's activation position

The result:

A completed underground tunnel.

---

# Descensus

## Meaning

Descensus means:

- descent
- going downward

## Purpose

Descensus is the vertical shaft creation Mutare block.

---

## Functionality

When activated:

Descensus digs downward one block wide until reaching bedrock.

The surrounding four blocks are replaced with stone.

---

## Protected Blocks

Descensus does not replace valuable blocks.

Examples:

- ores
- configured valuable resources

---

# Pons

## Meaning

Pons means:

- bridge

## Purpose

Pons is the bridge creation Mutare block.

---

## Functionality

When activated:

Pons creates a protected bridge structure.

The generated structure includes:

- bridge floor
- ceiling
- walls
- hollow windows
- wall torches

---

## Primary Use

Pons is designed for dangerous environments.

Examples:

- Nether lava lakes
- dangerous terrain crossings

The player can safely travel without worrying about:

- lava
- Ghast attacks
- unsafe terrain

---

# Vastatio

## Meaning

Vastatio means:

- devastation
- laying waste

## Purpose

Vastatio is the chunk transformation Mutare block.

---

## Functionality

When activated:

Vastatio clears an entire chunk downward until bedrock.

All removed blocks are dropped at the player's activation position.

---

## Protected Blocks

Vastatio does not destroy configured valuable blocks.

Examples:

- ores
- lapis blocks
- important resources

---

# Relationship With Other Mods

## Materia

[[Materia]] provides:

- materials
- Exodium
- crafting resources

---

## Virtus

Provides:

- ability activation
- transformation activation

Mutare requires Virtus to convert Mutatio into functional blocks.

---

## Arma

[[Arma]] provides:

- tools

Mutare complements tools by removing large repetitive environmental tasks.

---

## Praxis

[[Praxis]] provides:

- player abilities
- special utilities

Mutare changes the world.

Praxis changes the player.

---

## Machinum

Machinum provides:

- machines
- industrial processing

Mutare remains independent.

It does not require machinery progression.

---

## Potestas

Potestas provides:

- energy generation
- energy storage

Mutare does not require energy.
