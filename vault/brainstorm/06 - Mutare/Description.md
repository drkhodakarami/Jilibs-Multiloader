# Mutare

**"Mutare"** (pronounced *moo-TAH-reh*) — Latin for "to change, transform, alter — reshaping one state into another."

Mutare doesn't just add blocks. It gives you the power to reshape the world itself.

## What's Inside

**World Transformation Blocks (created through Mutatio + Virtus activation):**
- **Fontis** — Generate water from nothing
- **Siccus** — Remove water from an area
- **Calor** — Generate lava sources
- **Siccatio** — Drain lava from an area
- **Fodina** — Horizontal tunnel excavation (collects all drops automatically)
- **Descensus** — Vertical shaft straight to bedrock (preserves valuable ores)
- **Pons** — Protected bridge blocks — cross Nether lava lakes safely
- **Vastatio** — Clear entire chunks down to bedrock (ores preserved, everything else drops)

**Two Production Paths:**
- **Without Virtus:** Craft 1 block per Mutatio through standard recipes
- **With Virtus:** Get 4 blocks from the same Mutatio — mastery rewarded

## How It Fits

Mutare depends on **Materia** and connects deeply with **Virtus** (the activation system). It belongs to **Ars Vitae** (survival utilities) and **Ars Mundi** (full potential).

## Why You Want It

Tunneling through mountains, clearing land for mega-builds, managing fluids across massive projects — Mutare turns Minecraft's most tedious terrain work into single-block solutions. The world bends to your will.
