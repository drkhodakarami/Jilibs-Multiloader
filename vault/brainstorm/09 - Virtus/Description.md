# Virtus

**"Virtus"** (pronounced *im-PEH-tus*) — Latin for "impulse, driving force, momentum — the spark that awakens hidden potential."

Virtus doesn't create power. It reveals power that already exists inside everything around you.

## What's Inside

**The Helios Altar (Central):**
The centerpiece of the activation structure. Place the object you want to transform here.

**The Selene Altars (8 Surrounding):**
Hold the ingredients that unlock potential — Exodium, gems, rare materials. Each Selene contributes to the awakening.

**The Structure:**
A precise 5×5 layout with intentional gaps. When complete, the Helios altar changes appearance — visual proof that the structure is alive and ready.

**Activation Timing:**
- Mid-day recipes and mid-night recipes — when the world is at its most extreme, transformation becomes possible.

**What It Unlocks:**
- **Enhanced Arma tools** — Multi-block mining, special sword abilities
- **Enhanced Lorica armor** — Night vision, regeneration, flight, invisibility
- **Mutare blocks** — Dormant Mutatio becomes world-altering reality
- **Praxis abilities** — Flight through Exodium armor becomes possible

## How It Fits

Virtus is the **cross-cutting activation layer** of Ars Mundi. It belongs to the full **Ars Mundi** pack. It depends on **Materia** and interacts with nearly every other module.

## Why You Want It

Every powerful tool, every extraordinary armor set, every world-changing block in this ecosystem flows through Virtus. It's not magic. It's not technology. It's the moment potential becomes reality.
