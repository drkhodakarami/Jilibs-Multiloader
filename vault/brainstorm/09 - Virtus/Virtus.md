# Virtus - Mod Architecture Knowledge Base

## Overview

Virtus is the potential activation and enhancement mod of the [[Ars Mundi]] ecosystem.

Name:

Virtus

Pronunciation:

IM-pe-tus

Meaning:

Virtus comes from the Latin concept of:

- force
- momentum
- driving power
- impulse
- the force that causes something to happen

The name represents the purpose of this mod:

> Awakening the hidden potential inside objects and transforming them into something greater.

---

# Role in Ars Mundi

Ars Mundi is divided into different disciplines.

Each mod represents a specific area of mastery.

Virtus represents:

> The discipline of awakening potential.

While other mods create:

- materials
- tools
- armor
- machines
- storage
- transportation

Virtus provides the mechanism that unlocks their higher possibilities.

---

# Core Philosophy

## Potential Exists Before Activation

Many objects in Ars Mundi have a dormant capability.

Examples:

- tools can become more than normal tools
- armor can provide special abilities
- Mutatio can become world-changing blocks
- materials can be transformed into advanced creations

Virtus does not create power.

It reveals power that already exists.

The philosophy:

> Everything has potential. Virtus is the force that awakens it.

---

# Virtus Is Not A Technology System

Although the activation process has a structured layout, it is not a machine system.

It does not use:

- energy
- cables
- automation
- processing chains

It is an intentional transformation process.

---

# Virtus Is Not A Magic System

The system may appear ritual-like because of:

- altars
- particles
- activation effects
- celestial naming

However, it does not contain:

- spells
- mana
- wizard progression

The philosophy is:

> Mastery and understanding can create results that appear extraordinary.

---

# Helios

## Central Altar

Pronunciation:

HE-li-os

Meaning:

Helios is the Greek god of the Sun.

---

# Purpose

Helios is the central activation altar of Virtus.

The item, tool, armor, or intermediate object being transformed is placed inside Helios.

---

# Helios Functionality

Helios contains the central object during activation.

Possible central objects:

- [[Arma]] tools
- [[Lorica]] armor-related transformations
- Mutatio from [[Mutare]]
- future compatible objects

---

# Activation State

Helios only becomes active when the correct structure is completed.

If we mark Helios as "+" and Selene as "-" and empty blocks as "O",

The required structure:

```
-O-O-
OOOOO
-O+O_
OOOOO
-O-O-
```

8 Selene Altars, surrounding central Helios one with one empty block between each altar!

The eight Selene altars must be correctly positioned around Helios.

---

# Helios Visual Feedback

When the structure is correct:

Helios changes appearance to indicate readiness.

The player can visually identify that:

- the structure is valid
- activation is possible

---

# Selene

## Surrounding Altars

Pronunciation:

SE-le-ne

Meaning:

Selene is the Greek goddess of the Moon.

---

# Purpose

Selene altars surround Helios and provide the required ingredients for activation.

---

# Selene Functionality

Each Selene altar holds one or more required ingredients.

The ingredients depend on the transformation recipe.

Examples:

- Exodium [[1.2 - Alloys and Ingots]]
- gems [[1.1 - Gems]]
- special materials
- transformation components

---

# Activation Structure

The complete Virtus structure requires:

- one Helios altar in the center
- eight Selene altars surrounding it
- one block gap between the center and outer altars

Example:

```
-O-O-
OOOOO
-O+O_
OOOOO
-O-O-
```

Where:

```
+ = Helios
- = Selene
O = Empty blocks
```


---

# Activation Process

The activation process follows these steps:

## Step 1

The player places the object to transform inside Helios.

---

## Step 2

The player places all required ingredients inside Selene altars.

---

## Step 3

The structure is validated.

If the structure is incorrect:

- Helios remains inactive.

---

## Step 4

When activation begins at proper time (mid day or mid night based on the need):

Particles travel from Selene altars toward Helios.

The surrounding ingredients provide their potential to the center.

---

## Step 5

After the activation duration completes:

The transformation finishes.

The new item or block becomes available.

---

# Virtus Usage In The Ecosystem

Virtus is a dependency for systems that require awakening or enhancement.

---

# Arma Relationship

Arma tools have two states.

## Normal State

Without Virtus:

- tools behave like vanilla tools
- hammers behave like pickaxes
- excavators behave like shovels
- no special abilities exist

---

## Enhanced State

With Virtus:

Tools gain their special abilities.

Examples:

- hammer multi-block breaking
- excavator multi-block breaking
- special sword abilities

---

# Lorica Relationship

Lorica armor has two states.

## Normal State

Without Virtus:

Armor only provides:

- protection
- improved statistics

---

## Enhanced State

With Virtus:

Armor provides:

- individual armor piece abilities
- full set bonuses

Examples:

- regeneration
- night vision
- flight capability
- special player effects

---

# Mutare Relationship

Mutare will have default shaped recipe. when Virtus is present, those will become nullified and Virtus mechanics will be used.

The process:

```
Mutatio

↓

Helios + Selene Activation

↓

Mutare Block
```


Without Virtus:

Mutatio will be used as a shaped recipe ingredient for mutare block generation that will create

- Fontis
- Siccus
- Calor
- Siccatio
- Fodina
- Descensus
- Pons
- Vastatio

With Virtus:

It becomes:

- Fontis
- Siccus
- Calor
- Siccatio
- Fodina
- Descensus
- Pons
- Vastatio

The default, non Virtus mechanics, will generate less mutare blocks (1 vs 4).
