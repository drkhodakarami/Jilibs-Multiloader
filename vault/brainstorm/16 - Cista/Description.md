# Cista

**"Cista"** (pronounced *KIS-ta*) — Latin for "chest, basket, container — a place where your most valuable things live safely."

When your inventory overflows and shulker boxes aren't enough, Cista provides storage that scales with your ambition.

## What's Inside

**Advanced Storage Blocks:**
- Storage capacity far exceeding vanilla chests
- Shulker-like behavior — contents survive when the block is broken
- Organized, accessible, and reliable

**Preservation:**
- Your items stay safe inside even when you pick up the block
- Move your entire storage setup without losing a single item
- No more sorting many double chests when you relocate

**Future Expansion:**
- Fluid, gas, heat tank support
- Pressure, heat reservoir support
- Integration with Fistula transport networks
- Smart storage features to come

**Stationary Focus:**
Cista is your base storage. It sits in your base and holds your world. For mobile item / fluid storage, see Peram or Vas.

## How It Fits

Cista depends on **Materia**. It belongs to both **Ars Vitae** and **Ars Mundi**. It pairs with **Fistula** for automated input/output in the full Ars Mundi setup.

## Why You Want It

Every builder knows the pain of overflowing chests. Cista gives you storage blocks that match the scale of your projects — hold more, break without losing, and organize your world your way.
