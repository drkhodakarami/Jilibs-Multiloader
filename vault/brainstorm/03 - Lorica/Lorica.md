# Lorica - Mod Architecture Knowledge Base

## Overview

Lorica is the armor-focused mod of the [[Ars Mundi]] ecosystem.

Name:

Lorica

Pronunciation:

LO-ri-ka

Meaning:

Lorica comes from Latin and refers to:

- armor
- protective covering
- defensive equipment

Historically, a lorica was a type of armor worn by Roman soldiers.

The name represents the purpose of this mod:

> Protection, endurance, and survival through advanced equipment.

Where [[Arma]] represents the player's ability to act upon the world, Lorica represents the player's ability to withstand the world.

---

# Role in Ars Mundi

Ars Mundi is divided into different disciplines.

Each mod represents a specific area of mastery.

Lorica represents:

> The discipline of protection and personal defense.

[[Materia]] provides:

- materials
- gems
- exotic resources

Lorica transforms those materials into:

- helmets
- chestplates
- leggings
- boots

Lorica answers:

> How does the player protect themselves while interacting with the world?

---

# Core Philosophy

## Armor Is More Than Protection

In vanilla Minecraft, armor primarily represents:

- damage reduction
- durability
- progression

Lorica expands this idea.

Armor becomes a personal enhancement system.

The armor does not simply protect the player.

It improves the player's relationship with the environment.

Examples:

- seeing in darkness
- surviving extreme environments
- improving movement
- enhancing combat capability
- gaining supernatural-looking abilities

These abilities are not magic.

They represent advanced material properties unlocked through refinement.

---

# Lorica Is Not Virtus

A critical design separation exists between Lorica and [[Virtus]].

Lorica provides:

- armor pieces
- material compatibility
- armor statistics

Virtus provides:

- ability activation
- hidden potential unlocking
- special effects

Without Virtus:

Armor behaves normally.

With Virtus:

Armor abilities become active.

---

# Default Armor Philosophy

All Lorica armor has two states.

---

# Normal State

This is the default state when armor is crafted.

The armor provides:

- protection
- durability
- improved statistics

The player does not receive special effects.

Examples:

A Carminis chestplate:

- provides armor protection
- provides better material statistics

It does not automatically provide:

- regeneration
- resistance
- other abilities

---

# Enhanced State

After Virtus enhancement:

The armor unlocks its hidden potential.

Individual armor pieces provide effects.

A complete matching armor set provides additional bonuses.

The effects remain active as long as:

- the enhanced armor piece is equipped
- the required armor configuration remains valid

Removing or changing equipment removes the active benefits.

# Effect Activation System

Armor abilities are not permanently embedded into the armor.

The system works through Virtus.

Process:

Material acquisition

↓

Armor crafting

↓

Virtus enhancement

↓

Armor ability activation

↓

Player receives effects while equipped

---

# Full Set Philosophy

Individual armor pieces provide specialized abilities.

A complete matching set provides additional mastery.

This creates two gameplay styles:

## Mixed Armor

Players can combine different armor pieces.

They receive individual effects.

Example:

Carminis helmet + Glaucus boots

provides:

- Saturation
- Invisibility

but does not activate a full set bonus.

---

## Complete Armor Set

Players wearing a full matching set unlock:

- all individual effects
- additional set bonus

This rewards dedication to a material identity.

---

# Relationship With Virtus

Lorica depends on Virtus for advanced functionality.

Lorica:

Virtus:

Unlocks potential.

Material collection

↓

Armor creation

↓

Enhancement

↓

Special abilities

---

# Relationship With Praxis

Exodium armor introduces a special relationship with Praxis.

[[Praxis]] provides:

- custom player abilities
- flight system (Levitas)
- wall climb system (gravitas)

Lorica provides:

- the equipment that can unlock those abilities

Example:

Exodium full armor set

↓

Activates flight effect

↓

Praxis handles flight behavior

---

# Final Philosophy

Lorica represents the second layer of player mastery.

First, the player gains tools.

Then, the player gains protection.

The world becomes dangerous.

The player becomes prepared.

The armor does not make the player invincible.

It represents the evolution from survivor to master.
