# Lorica

**"Lorica"** (pronounced *loh-REE-ka*) — Latin for "armor, protective covering — the shield between you and everything trying to kill you."

Named after the legendary armor worn by Roman soldiers, Lorica doesn't just protect you. It transforms what protection means in Minecraft.

## What's Inside

**Full Armor Sets:**
- Emerald, Amethyst, Copper, Carminis, Glaucus, Flavum, and Exodium armor sets
- Each material brings its own identity and protective character

**Per-Piece Enhancement Effects (through Virtus):**
- **Night Vision** — See clearly in the darkest caves
- **Regeneration** — Recover health in the heat of battle
- **Flight** — Soar through the skies in full Exodium armor
- **Invisibility** — Vanish from sight entirely

**Full Set Bonuses:**
Wear a complete set and unlock combined effects that make each material choice feel meaningful and powerful.

**Two States:**
Like Arma tools, Lorica armor starts in *Normal* mode (solid protection + stats) and can be activated to *Enhanced* mode through Virtus — where each piece becomes a personal superpower.

## How It Fits

Lorica depends on **Materia** for its materials and gems. It belongs to both **Ars Vitae** and **Ars Mundi**. Pair it with **Virtus** to unlock the full potential of your armor. The Exodium full set + Virtus = flight — the peak reward of the entire ecosystem.

## Why You Want It

In vanilla Minecraft, armor just reduces damage numbers. In Lorica, your armor becomes part of who you are — granting abilities that change how you explore, fight, and experience the world.
