# Arma

**"Arma"** (pronounced *AR-mah*) — Latin for "tool, instrument, implement — the extension of a hand that shapes the world."

Tools are the first thing a player reaches for. Arma takes that simple instinct and pushes it to its absolute limit.

## What's Inside

**Complete Tool Collection:**
- Swords, Pickaxes, Shovels, Axes, Hoes — the essentials, elevated
- **Hammers** — Devastating mining tools that break multiple blocks at once
- **Excavators** — Earth-moving powerhouses for clearing terrain fast

**Material Tiers with Personality:**
Craft your tools from vanilla materials (Wood through Netherite) or push into exotic territory with Carminis, Glaucus, Flavum, and Exodium — each gem-touched tool carries unique properties.

**Enhanced Abilities (through Virtus activation):**
- **Carminis Sword** — Cooked meat drops from every kill. Never go hungry again.
- **Glaucus Sword** — Massive knockback sends enemies flying.
- **Flavum Sword** — Blinding strikes that leave foes disoriented.
- **Copper Sword** — Poisons targets and drops rotten flesh.
- **Exodium Sword** — The ultimate exotic weapon with untold potential.

**Two States:**
Every Arma tool exists in *Normal* mode (solid vanilla-style performance) and *Enhanced* mode (unlocked through Virtus — where tools become extraordinary).

## How It Fits

Arma depends on **Materia** for its materials. It belongs to both **Ars Vitae** and **Ars Mundi** packs. When paired with **Virtus**, tools transcend their normal limits.

## Why You Want It

A pickaxe is just a pickaxe — until it's not. Arma transforms Minecraft tools from simple utilities into instruments of mastery. The moment you activate your first Enhanced tool, you'll never look back.
