# Praxis - Mod Architecture Knowledge Base

## Overview

Praxis is the player utility and personal capability expansion mod of the [[Ars Mundi]] ecosystem.

Name:

Praxis

Pronunciation:

PRAK-sis

Meaning:

Praxis comes from the Latin concept of:

- action
- practice
- application
- putting knowledge into use

The name represents the purpose of this mod:

> Turning knowledge and mastery into direct action.

Where:

[[Materia]] represents materials.

[[Arma]] represents tools.

[[Lorica]] represents protection.

Praxis represents what the player can personally achieve.

---

# Role in Ars Mundi

Ars Mundi is divided into different disciplines.

Each mod focuses on a specific area of mastery.

Praxis represents:

> Personal abilities, movement, and direct interaction with the world.

Praxis is not about machines.

Praxis is not about automation.

Praxis is about removing unnecessary limitations from the player.

---

# Core Philosophy

## The Player Is the Final System

Many mods focus on changing the world around the player.

They add:

- machines
- automation
- infrastructure
- processing systems

Praxis focuses on changing what the player can do.

Examples:

- traveling instantly between important locations
- moving entities efficiently
- having permanent access to basic survival resources
- gaining advanced movement abilities

The player becomes the ultimate tool.

---

# Praxis Is Not a Technology Mod

Praxis does not contain:

- machines
- factories
- energy systems
- cables
- processors
- industrial production

Those belong to:

- Machinum
- Potestas
- Fistula

Praxis abilities are personal.

---

# Praxis Is Not Virtus

Praxis and [[Virtus]] work together but have different responsibilities.

Praxis provides:

- player effects
- personal abilities
- utility items

Virtus provides:

- ability activation
- equipment enhancement
- unlocking hidden potential

Example:

Exodium armor provides the possibility of flight. [[1.2 - Alloys and Ingots]]

Praxis provides the flight behavior.

Virtus activates the connection.

---

# Tools

Praxis provides three unique utility tools.

---

# Iterum

## Meaning

Iterum means:

- again
- repeated
- returning

## Purpose

Iterum is a personal teleportation tool.

It allows the player to travel between registered locations.

---

## Features

Iterum can:

- store player destinations
- teleport the player
- work across dimensions

Examples:

The player can register a single location, this can be his:

- home
- mining base
- farming area
- Nether base
- End base

and instantly travel between current location and registered one.

---

## Design Philosophy

Iterum represents mastery over distance.

It does not create a transportation network.

It does not replace exploration.

It removes repetitive travel after the player has already far away in exploration and survived the adventure.

---

# Locus

## Meaning

Locus means:

- place
- location
- position

## Purpose

Locus is an entity teleportation tool.

It allows the player to move living entities to registered locations.

Affected entities include:

- animals
- passive mobs
- hostile mobs

---

## Features

Locus can:

- store entity destinations
- teleport selected entities
- move creatures efficiently

Examples:

- moving animals to a farm
- relocating mobs for controlled systems
- organizing creatures around the player's world

---

## Limitations

Locus does not work between dimensions.

Entities remain inside their current dimension.

Reason:

Cross-dimensional movement belongs to player-level traversal and creates different gameplay implications.

---

## Design Philosophy

Locus represents control over location.

It does not manipulate life.

It only changes where existing entities are positioned.

---

# Chalice

## Meaning

A chalice is a vessel or container.

## Purpose

Chalice is an infinite water source tool.

It behaves like a normal bucket.

However:

- collecting water does not empty it
- the player can place water repeatedly
- no additional water collection is required

---

## Design Philosophy

Chalice removes repetitive survival maintenance.

Water is an essential resource, but repeatedly managing basic resources does not represent meaningful progression.

Chalice represents mastery over a simple necessity.

---

# Status Effects

Praxis provides custom player status effects.

---

# Levitas

## Description

Flight is a custom player status effect.

Behavior:

- provides creative-like flight
- allows free vertical movement
- removes normal movement limitations

The behavior is similar to Minecraft creative mode flight.

---

## Acquisition

Flight is intentionally restricted.

The only normal way to obtain it is:

Enhanced Full Exodium Armor Set

provided by:

[[Lorica]] + Virtus

---

## Design Philosophy

Flight represents the highest level of player mastery.

It is not a normal convenience ability.

It is the reward for reaching the peak of the ecosystem.

---

# Gravitas

## Meaning

Gravitas means:

- weight
- influence
- seriousness
- physical presence

## Description

Gravitas is a movement enhancement effect that allows the player to climb vertical surfaces.

The effect creates the feeling that the player has control over their physical relationship with the world.

---

## Behavior

While affected by Gravitas:

- the player can move vertically upward on walls
- normal jumping is no longer required for vertical climbing

The player gains wall-climbing capability.

---

## Acquisition

Gravitas can be obtained through:

## Leather Boots

Leather boots naturally provide access to the effect.

provided by:

[[Lorica]] + Virtus

OR

## Gravitas Potion

Praxis provides a potion that grants Gravitas temporarily.

---

## Design Philosophy

Gravitas represents physical adaptation.

The player is not becoming a spider.

The player is gaining control over movement and attachment.
