# Praxis

**"Praxis"** (pronounced *PRAK-sis*) — Latin for "action, practice, application — the moment knowledge becomes real."

Where other mods change the world around you, Praxis changes what *you* can do.

## What's Inside

**Personal Teleportation:**
- **Iterum** — Register a location and return to it instantly. Works across dimensions.

**Entity Teleportation:**
- **Locus** — Move animals and mobs between locations. Never chase a wandering cow again.

**Infinite Resources:**
- **Chalice** — An infinite water bucket. Fill cauldrons, create sources, hydrate farms — forever.

**Advanced Movement:**
- **Levitas** — Creative-like flight, unlocked through the Exodium full armor set and Virtus activation.
- **Gravitas** — Climb any surface. Walk up walls like a spider. Unlocked through leather boots + Virtus or a potion.

## How It Fits

Praxis depends on **Materia**. It belongs to both **Ars Vitae** and **Ars Mundi**. Flight and wall-climbing are the ultimate player ability rewards — earned through mastery, not cheats.

## Why You Want It

Every Minecraft player has felt the frustration of slow travel, lost mobs, and bucket management. Praxis eliminates those pain points and replaces them with extraordinary personal capability. The player becomes the ultimate tool.
