# Tractus

**"Tractus"** (pronounced *TRAK-tus*) — Latin for "pulling, drawing, extraction — drawing resources from where they hide to where you need them."

Minecraft's fluids sit stubbornly in those cauldrons, waiting to be collected. Tractus pulls them out — no factory required.

## What's Inside

**The Extraction Block:**
- Automatically searches for nearby water and lava source  in cauldrons
- Extracts fluid from cauldrons (water, lava, and even powdered snow)
- Stores fluid internally for immediate use
- Emits a redstone pulse when extraction completes
- No energy required — works in pure survival mode

**Simple Pipeline:**
Tractus extracts → Vas stores → Fistula transports. Each mod handles one step of the chain.

## How It Fits

Tractus depends on **Materia**. It belongs to both **Ars Vitae** and **Ars Mundi**. It's intentionally focused — one block that does extraction, nothing more. Pair it with **Vas** for storage and **Fistula** for transport.

## Why You Want It

Moving water bucket by bucket. Scooping lava one bucket at a time. Tractus eliminates the repetition. Place it, let it work, and focus on what actually matters — your next build.
