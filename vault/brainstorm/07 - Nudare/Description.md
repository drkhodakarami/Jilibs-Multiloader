# Nudare

**"Nudare"** (pronounced *noo-DAH-reh*) — Latin for "to strip, to expose, to reveal the true form hidden beneath the surface."

Some problems don't need a factory. They need a simple, elegant solution.

## What's Inside

**The Log Stripper Block:**
- Place and feed it with a stack of logs
- It automatically strips every log in contact
- A full stack of logs stripped in under 20 seconds
- No energy required. No complex setup. Just results.

**How It Works:**
1. Place the Nudare block in the world, a hopper on top
2. Watch it work through the stack automatically
3. Collect your stripped logs
4. Done.

**Complements Your Axe:**
Your axe still harvests logs. Nudare handles the tedious stripping part. Together, they make wood preparation effortless.

## How It Fits

Nudare depends on **Materia**. It belongs to both **Ars Vitae** and **Ars Mundi**. It's intentionally minimal — one block, one job, done perfectly.

## Why You Want It

You've stripped hundreds of logs by hand for building projects. Nudare ends that forever. Small problem? Small solution. Beautiful.
