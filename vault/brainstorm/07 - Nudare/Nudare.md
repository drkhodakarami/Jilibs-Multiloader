# Nudare - Mod Architecture Knowledge Base

## Overview

Nudare is a specialized single-block utility mod in the [[Ars Mundi]] ecosystem.

Name:

Nudare

Pronunciation:

nu-DA-re

Meaning:

Nudare comes from the Latin concept of:

- stripping
- exposing
- uncovering
- removing an outer layer

The name represents the purpose of this mod:

> Revealing the true form hidden beneath the surface.

Nudare focuses on one specific interaction:

Transforming logs into their stripped versions automatically.

---

# Role in Ars Mundi

Ars Mundi is divided into different disciplines.

Each mod represents a specific area of mastery.

Nudare represents:

> The discipline of material preparation.

Unlike large automation systems, Nudare focuses on a single repetitive action that exists throughout normal Minecraft gameplay.

Nudare answers:

> How can simple but tedious preparation steps be improved without introducing a full industrial system?

---

# Core Philosophy

## Small Problems Deserve Small Solutions

Minecraft contains many repetitive actions.

Some of them are not difficult.

They are simply time-consuming.

One example:

Stripping wood logs.

Normally, the player must:

1. Hold an axe.
2. Approach every log.
3. Right-click every individual block.
4. Repeat the process for large building projects.

This is not a challenge.

It is repetition.

Nudare exists to remove this friction.

---

# Nudare Is Not a Machine Mod

Although Nudare adds a block, it is intentionally not part of Machinum.

It does not contain:

- automation networks
- energy systems
- processors
- production chains
- industrial mechanics

The block exists as a focused utility.

---

# Nudare Is Not Automation

Nudare does not replace player interaction.

The player still needs to:

- collect logs
- provide materials
- interact with the block

It only removes unnecessary repetition.

---

# The Nudare Block

Nudare adds a single block.

Purpose:

Automatically convert normal logs into stripped logs.

---

# Functionality

The Nudare block:

- accepts wood logs
- removes their outer bark layer
- produces stripped versions

Examples:

Input:

Oak Log

Output:

Stripped Oak Log


Input:

Spruce Log

Output:

Stripped Spruce Log

---

# Processing

The block does not require:

- energy
- fuel
- power generation
- external systems

It is intentionally independent.

A player can use Nudare with only:

- Materia
- Arma
- Lorica
- Cultus
- other non-tech Ars Vitae modules

---

# Speed

The purpose of Nudare is rapid preparation.

A full stack of logs can be processed in less than 20 seconds.

The player does not need to manually strip hundreds of logs for large construction projects.

---

# Design Philosophy

## Efficiency Without Complexity

Many technology mods solve simple problems by introducing:

- machines
- power
- cables
- processing chains

Nudare intentionally avoids this.

The philosophy is:

> A simple problem should have a simple solution.

The player should not need a factory to remove bark from wood.

---
