# Vas

**"Vas"** (pronounced *VAHS*) — Latin for "vessel, container, receptacle — something built to hold what cannot hold itself."

In Minecraft, solid items sit in inventories. Fluids and gases need something more. Vas provides the vessels.

## What's Inside

**Fluid Containers:**
- Store water, lava, and other fluids in portable vessels
- Transport fluids between locations
- Fill and empty through standard Minecraft interactions

**Gas Containers:**
- Contain and store gaseous substances
- Enable gas-based systems in the ecosystem

**Battery Items:**
- Portable energy storage
- Carry power wherever you go
- Charge at Potestas generators, discharge at Machinum machines

**What Vas Is NOT:**
- Not a chest or tank (that's Cista)
- Not a backpack (that's Peram)
- Not a transport pipe (that's Fistula)
- Vas is purely about *containing* what needs containing.

## How It Fits

Vas depends on **Materia**. It belongs to **Ars Mundi**. It sits between Tractus (extraction) and Fistula (transport) — collect it, contain it, move it.

## Why You Want It

Fluids, gases, portable energy — these need homes. Vas gives them portable homes that you can carry. Simple, essential, and exactly what the ecosystem needs to complete the resource chain.
