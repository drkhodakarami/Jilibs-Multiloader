# Regnum - Mod Architecture Knowledge Base

## Overview

Regnum is the dimension and realm-focused mod of the [Ars Mundi](https://chatgpt.com/c/Ars%20Mundi) ecosystem.

Name:

Regnum

Pronunciation:

REG-num

Meaning:

Regnum comes from Latin and refers to:

- kingdom
    
- realm
    
- dominion
    
- territory
    
- domain
    

The name represents the purpose of this mod:

> Defining the realms in which the world of Ars Mundi exists.

Regnum is responsible for creating and defining the dimensions of the ecosystem.

It establishes the different realms, their environments, and the rules that make each realm distinct.

---

# Role in Ars Mundi

Ars Mundi is divided into different disciplines.

Each mod represents a specific area of mastery.

Regnum represents:

> The discipline of realms, dimensions, and worlds.

Regnum provides:

- dimensions
    
- dimension environments
    
- realm-specific world generation
    
- dimension-specific rules
    
- access to additional realms
    

Regnum answers:

> Where does the world of Ars Mundi exist, and what other realms lie beyond it?

---

# Core Philosophy

## The World Should Be Larger Than One Dimension

Minecraft already establishes the idea that the world can extend beyond the Overworld.

The Nether and the End demonstrate that different dimensions can represent completely different environments and gameplay experiences.

Regnum expands this philosophy for Ars Mundi.

Each dimension should feel like:

- a distinct realm
    
- a different environment
    
- a unique gameplay space
    
- a place with its own identity
    

A new dimension should not simply be:

> The Overworld with a different sky.

It should feel like entering another realm.

---

# Dimensions Are Realms

Regnum treats dimensions as **realms**, rather than simply alternate maps.

A realm can have its own:

- terrain
    
- atmosphere
    
- lighting
    
- environmental conditions
    
- structures
    
- resources
    
- creatures
    
- hazards
    
- gameplay rules
    
- progression
    

This allows each dimension to have a purpose within the larger Ars Mundi world.

---

# Existing Dimensions

Minecraft already provides several dimensions.

These include:

- Overworld
    
- Nether
    
- End
    

Regnum does not need to replace the identity of vanilla dimensions.

Instead, it establishes the additional dimensions that belong to Ars Mundi.

The existing vanilla dimensions remain important parts of the overall world.

---

# Ars Mundi Dimensions

Regnum is responsible for the dimensions that were defined for the Ars Mundi ecosystem.

These dimensions can be expanded over time.

The initial dimension list should therefore be treated as the foundation rather than a permanent limitation.

Future realms can be introduced when they have a meaningful purpose.

---

# Dimension Identity

Every Regnum dimension should have a strong identity.

A dimension should be distinguishable through more than its color palette.

Its identity should come from a combination of:

- environment
    
- terrain
    
- resources
    
- hazards
    
- atmosphere
    
- progression
    
- structures
    
- gameplay mechanics
    

The player should be able to recognize where they are because the realm itself communicates its identity.

---

# Dimension Progression

Dimensions can represent different stages of player progression.

A realm may be:

- accessible early
    
- accessible after obtaining specific materials
    
- accessible after completing a particular progression
    
- dangerous despite being accessible
    
- intended for late-game exploration
    

Regnum defines the realm.

Other mods can define the systems that make entering or surviving that realm meaningful.

---

# Dimension Access

Regnum is responsible for defining dimensions.

It does not necessarily need to own every mechanism used to access them.

A dimension may be accessed through:

- portals
    
- structures
    
- special items
    
- abilities
    
- rituals
    
- future ecosystem mechanics
    

The access method can depend on the nature of the realm.

This prevents Regnum from becoming responsible for every dimension-related gameplay mechanic.

---

# Relationship With Praxis

[Praxis](https://chatgpt.com/c/Praxis) provides player-oriented utility and abilities.

One important example is **Iterum**, the player teleporter.

Because Iterum can teleport players between dimensions, it naturally interacts with Regnum.

Regnum defines:

> Where the player can go.

Praxis provides:

> A tool that can move the player there.

This keeps the two systems separate.

---

# Relationship With Mutare

[Mutare](https://chatgpt.com/c/Mutare) contains blocks capable of manipulating the world.

Some Mutare systems can have dimension-specific applications.

For example, a Mutare mechanism may behave differently depending on the realm in which it is activated.

Regnum defines the realm and its rules.

Mutare operates within that realm.

---

# Relationship With Altum

[Altum](https://chatgpt.com/c/Altum) is responsible for blocks related to:

- void
    
- sky
    
- vertical space
    
- special placement environments
    

Regnum dimensions can provide environments where these systems become particularly relevant.

For example:

A dimension with large areas of void may make **Angel Blocks** especially useful.

The relationship is:

> Regnum creates the realm.

> Altum provides tools for interacting with its unique spatial environments.

---

# Relationship With Materia

[Materia](https://chatgpt.com/c/Materia) defines the foundational materials used throughout Ars Mundi.

Different Regnum dimensions can introduce new opportunities to obtain or use these materials.

A dimension may contain:

- existing resources
    
- rare materials
    
- dimension-specific resources
    
- special variants
    

However, Regnum itself should remain focused on the realm.

Material definitions should remain within the appropriate material systems whenever possible.

---

# Relationship With Machinum

[Machinum](https://chatgpt.com/c/Machinum) provides machines and processing systems.

Different dimensions can provide different environments for these machines.

A dimension may introduce:

- unique resources
    
- environmental challenges
    
- different processing opportunities
    
- special machine interactions
    

Regnum defines the environment.

Machinum provides the machines that operate within it.

---

# Relationship With Potestas

[Potestas](https://chatgpt.com/c/Potestas) provides energy generation and storage.

Different dimensions may naturally support different energy-generation strategies.

For example, environmental conditions could make certain generators more appropriate in certain realms.

Regnum establishes the environment.

Potestas determines how the player can exploit that environment for energy.

---

# Relationship With Tractus

[Tractus](https://chatgpt.com/c/Tractus) provides fluid extraction.

Different dimensions can contain different fluids or fluid environments.

Tractus can therefore interact with those dimension-specific environments.

Regnum defines:

> What exists in the realm.

Tractus defines:

> How the player extracts it.

---

# Relationship With Fistula

[Fistula](https://chatgpt.com/c/Fistula) provides transportation through pipes and cables.

Dimension boundaries can affect transportation systems.

Regnum therefore establishes the worlds in which Fistula networks operate.

Cross-dimensional transportation, if introduced, should be explicitly designed rather than assumed.

A Fistula network should not automatically bypass the meaning of separate dimensions.

---

# Relationship With Lorica

[Lorica](https://chatgpt.com/c/Lorica) provides armor and environmental protection.

Different dimensions can contain different environmental dangers.

This gives dimension-specific armor abilities and material progression a meaningful purpose.

Regnum creates the danger.

Lorica helps the player survive it.

---

# Relationship With Arma

[Arma](https://chatgpt.com/c/Arma) provides tools and weapons.

Different dimensions can contain:

- unique blocks
    
- hostile creatures
    
- environmental hazards
    
- special resources
    

Arma allows the player to interact with and survive those challenges.

Regnum provides the environment in which those tools are used.

---

# Dimension-Specific Content

A Regnum dimension may eventually contain unique content such as:

- blocks
    
- resources
    
- structures
    
- creatures
    
- plants
    
- environmental effects
    
- terrain formations
    

However, these systems should be assigned to the appropriate specialized mods when practical.

Regnum should primarily define the **realm itself**.

---

# Dimension Design Philosophy

## Every Dimension Needs A Reason To Exist

A new dimension should not be added simply because another dimension is possible.

It should answer:

> Why should the player go there?

A dimension should provide something meaningful.

That could be:

- exploration
    
- resources
    
- progression
    
- danger
    
- unique mechanics
    
- unique environments
    
- lore
    
- access to another part of the ecosystem
    

A dimension without a purpose risks becoming nothing more than another empty world.

---

# Dimensions Should Feel Different

Regnum should avoid creating dimensions that are functionally identical.

Two realms may share some mechanics, but they should have meaningful differences in:

- atmosphere
    
- terrain
    
- hazards
    
- resources
    
- exploration
    
- gameplay
    

The goal is variety rather than quantity.

---

# Dimension Expansion

The initial collection of Regnum dimensions is not necessarily final.

New dimensions can be added as Ars Mundi expands.

Possible future realms can be introduced when they support:

- new gameplay
    
- new progression
    
- new mechanics
    
- new environments
    
- new stories
    
- new ecosystem relationships
    

Regnum therefore acts as the expandable dimensional foundation of Ars Mundi.

- exploration

In the complete Ars Mundi ecosystem, Regnum provides the **space in which the other disciplines exist**.

Materia provides the resources.

Arma provides the tools.

Lorica provides protection.

Praxis provides utility.

Machinum provides machines.

Potestas provides energy.

Fistula provides transportation.

Vas provides containment.

Mutare manipulates the world.

Regnum provides the realms in which all of these systems operate.

---

# Long-Term Philosophy

Regnum should remain an expandable framework for the worlds of Ars Mundi.

Every new dimension should be evaluated carefully.

The question should not be:

> Can we create another dimension?

The question should be:

> Does this dimension add something meaningful to Ars Mundi?

If it does, Regnum provides its foundation.

If it does not, another dimension should not exist simply for the sake of increasing the number of realms.

---

# Final Philosophy

Regnum represents the expansion of the world beyond a single realm.

The player begins in one world.

Eventually, they discover that the world is only part of something larger.

Each dimension becomes another chapter of exploration.

Another environment.

Another challenge.

Another place to master.

Regnum defines those places.

**Regnum — The Realms of Ars Mundi.**
