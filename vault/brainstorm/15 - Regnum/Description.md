# Regnum

**"Regnum"** (pronounced *REG-num*) — Latin for "kingdom, realm, dominion — the territories where your world exists."

Minecraft gave you the Overworld, the Nether, and the End. Regnum asks: what other realms could exist?

## What's Inside

**Custom Dimensions:**
- Entirely new worlds to explore
- Unique terrain generation and environmental rules
- Distinct gameplay experiences beyond vanilla dimensions

**Dimension-Specific Rules:**
- Each realm has its own physics, resources, and challenges
- New biomes, materials, and structures exclusive to each dimension
- Reason to explore beyond novelty — real gameplay rewards

**Portal Systems:**
- Access dimensions through specific portal mechanics
- Cross-dimensional travel using Ars Mundi's own systems
- Origo and Virtus keys gate access to specific realms

**World Generation:**
- Complete terrain and structure generation for each dimension
- Environment-specific resources that feed back into the ecosystem

## How It Fits

Regnum depends on **Virtus** and **Materia** (especially Exodium). It belongs to **Ars Mundi**. It's the endgame exploration layer — new worlds for players who've mastered the original.

## Why You Want It

You've conquered the Overworld. You've raided the End. Regnum opens doors to realms that feel genuinely new — not reskinned dimensions, but worlds with their own identity and purpose.
