# Fistula - Mod Architecture Knowledge Base

## Overview

Fistula is the transportation and transfer-focused mod of the [Ars Mundi](https://chatgpt.com/c/Ars%20Mundi) ecosystem.

Name:

Fistula

Pronunciation:

FIS-tu-la

Meaning:

Fistula comes from Latin and refers to:

- pipe
    
- tube
    
- conduit
    
- channel
    
- hollow passage
    

The name represents the purpose of this mod:

> Creating pathways through which materials, energy, and objects can travel.

Fistula provides the transportation infrastructure that connects different systems throughout Ars Mundi.

It includes:

- pipes
    
- tubes
    
- cables
    
- conveyor belts
    

Fistula answers:

> How do resources and other materials move from one place to another?

---

# Role in Ars Mundi

Ars Mundi is divided into different disciplines.

Each mod represents a specific area of mastery.

Fistula represents:

> The discipline of transportation, transfer, and connectivity.

Fistula provides the physical infrastructure required to move resources between systems.

This includes:

- fluid transportation
    
- item transportation
    
- energy transportation
    
- physical item movement
    

---

# Core Philosophy

## Resources Should Be Able To Move

As the player's infrastructure grows, resources must move between different locations.

A machine may produce something in one location.

A storage system may exist somewhere else.

A generator may produce energy far from the machine that requires it.

A fluid source may be separated from the tank that stores it.

Fistula connects these systems.

The fundamental philosophy is:

> Production is only useful when its results can reach where they are needed.

---

# Fistula Is Infrastructure

Fistula does not primarily generate resources.

It does not primarily process resources.

It does not primarily store resources.

It provides the infrastructure connecting those systems.

The basic relationship is:

> Other mods create, process, store, or consume.

> Fistula moves.

---

# Transportation Categories

Fistula contains several transportation systems.

These systems are designed for different types of materials and movement.

The primary categories are:

- pipes
    
- tubes
    
- cables
    
- conveyor belts
    

Each has a distinct purpose.

---

# Pipes

Pipes are intended primarily for transporting fluids.

They provide a connection between:

- fluid sources
    
- tanks
    
- machines
    
- other compatible fluid blocks
    

For example:

Tractus extracts a fluid.

↓

Fistula pipe transports it.

↓

Cista tank stores it.

The pipe itself does not generate or store the fluid.

---

# Tubes

Tubes are intended primarily for transporting items.

They allow items to move between compatible systems.

For example:

Cista storage

↓

Fistula tube

↓

Machinum machine

The tube provides the transportation path.

The systems connected to it determine what the item is and what happens to it.

---

# Cables

Cables provide energy transportation.

They connect:

- energy generators
    
- energy storage
    
- machines
    
- other compatible energy consumers
    

For example:

Potestas generator

↓

Fistula cable

↓

Machinum machine

The cable transports energy.

It does not generate the energy itself.

---

# Conveyor Belts

Conveyor belts provide visible, physical item transportation.

Unlike tubes, conveyor belts represent the movement of objects through the world itself.

They are intended for:

- factories
    
- processing lines
    
- visible transportation systems
    
- machine layouts
    
- industrial construction
    

A conveyor belt can make an automated production system physically visible.

---

# Tubes vs Conveyor Belts

Fistula intentionally supports both abstract and visible item transportation.

## Tubes

Tubes are:

- compact
    
- enclosed
    
- space-efficient
    
- suitable for long-distance transportation
    

They represent items moving through an enclosed transportation network.

---

## Conveyor Belts

Conveyor belts are:

- visible
    
- physical
    
- spatial
    
- suitable for factory layouts
    

They represent items physically moving through the factory.

The player can therefore choose between:

> Hidden transportation.

and:

> Visible transportation.

---

# Transportation Networks

Fistula systems can form networks.

A network can connect multiple:

- machines
    
- storage blocks
    
- generators
    
- tanks
    
- processing systems
    

The network should allow the player to design infrastructure rather than requiring every machine to connect directly to every other machine.

This becomes increasingly important as an Ars Mundi installation grows.

---

# Connection Philosophy

Transportation blocks should connect naturally to compatible systems.

For example:

A pipe should connect to:

- tanks
    
- fluid machines
    
- fluid extraction systems
    

A cable should connect to:

- generators
    
- energy storage
    
- energy-consuming machines
    

A tube should connect to:

- item storage
    
- item-processing machines
    
- compatible inventories
    

The transportation system should make these relationships understandable.

---

# Relationship With Cista

[Cista](https://chatgpt.com/c/Cista) provides stationary storage.

Fistula provides transportation to and from that storage.

The relationship is:

> Cista stores.

> Fistula transports.

For example:

Cista chest

↓

Fistula tube

↓

Machinum machine

A machine can therefore retrieve materials from storage without requiring the player to manually move every item.

---

# Relationship With Vas

[Vas](https://chatgpt.com/c/Vas) provides containment for fluids, gases, and other appropriate materials.

Fistula provides the pathways through which compatible materials can travel.

The relationship is:

> Vas contains.

> Fistula transports.

This allows contained materials to move through an infrastructure network.

---

# Relationship With Tractus

[Tractus](https://chatgpt.com/c/Tractus) provides fluid extraction.

Fistula provides fluid transportation.

A typical fluid system can therefore be:

Fluid source

↓

Tractus pump

↓

Fistula pipe

↓

Cista tank

Tractus performs the extraction.

Fistula performs the transportation.

Cista performs the storage.

---

# Relationship With Machinum

[Machinum](https://chatgpt.com/c/Machinum) provides machines and processing systems.

Fistula connects those machines together.

A production line can therefore become:

Cista

↓

Fistula tube

↓

Machinum processor

↓

Fistula tube

↓

Cista

Fistula acts as the infrastructure between the different stages of production.

---

# Relationship With Potestas

[Potestas](https://chatgpt.com/c/Potestas) provides energy generation and energy storage.

Fistula provides the cables that transport that energy.

A typical energy system can therefore be:

Solarium

↓

Fistula cable

↓

Machinum machine

The generator belongs to Potestas.

The cable belongs to Fistula.

The machine belongs to Machinum.

---

# Relationship With Materia

[Materia](https://chatgpt.com/c/Materia) provides the foundational resources of Ars Mundi.

Fistula allows those resources to move through the systems that process and use them.

Materia creates the material progression.

Fistula provides the infrastructure through which that progression can operate at scale.

---

# Relationship With Augmentum

[Augmentum](https://chatgpt.com/c/Augmentum) provides upgrades that can be applied to compatible systems throughout Ars Mundi.

Fistula transportation blocks may become compatible with appropriate upgrades.

For example, future upgrades could potentially affect:

- transfer speed
    
- capacity
    
- filtering
    
- routing
    
- efficiency
    

However, the transportation system itself remains part of Fistula.

The distinction is:

> Fistula provides transportation.

> Augmentum enhances compatible transportation systems.

---

# Transportation and Automation

Fistula is one of the systems that transforms individual machines into an automated infrastructure.

Without transportation:

A machine may require constant manual interaction.

With transportation:

Resources can automatically move between stages.

For example:

Resource storage

↓

Item transportation

↓

Processing

↓

Item transportation

↓

Output storage

Fistula therefore acts as one of the foundations of Ars Mundi automation.

---

# Visual Infrastructure

Fistula is not only functional.

Its transportation blocks should also contribute to the appearance of industrial and technical builds.

A developed factory can visibly communicate its infrastructure through:

- pipes
    
- tubes
    
- cables
    
- conveyor belts
    

This allows the player to see how their systems are connected.

The transportation network becomes part of the structure itself.

---

# Dimension Considerations

Fistula networks exist within the world and dimensions in which they are placed.

[Regnum](https://chatgpt.com/c/Regnum) defines the dimensions of Ars Mundi.

Fistula defines transportation within those environments.

Cross-dimensional transportation should not be assumed automatically.

If cross-dimensional transportation is introduced, it should be an intentional mechanic with clear limitations and progression.

Separate dimensions should retain meaning.

# Ars Vitae Relationship

Fistula primarily supports the technology-oriented side of Ars Mundi.

It can still provide useful infrastructure for Ars Vitae systems when transportation is required.

Its primary purpose, however, is to enable larger and more complex automated systems.

---

# Ars Mundi Relationship

In the complete Ars Mundi ecosystem, Fistula provides the **transportation layer**.

Materia provides resources.

Machinum processes them.

Potestas provides energy.

Tractus extracts fluids.

Cista stores materials.

Vas provides containment.

Fistula connects these systems.

Without transportation, each system remains isolated.

With Fistula, they become an interconnected infrastructure.

---

# Long-Term Philosophy

Fistula should remain focused on transportation.

Whenever a new feature is proposed, the question should be:

> Is this fundamentally about moving something from one location to another?

If yes:

It may belong in Fistula.

If it instead focuses on:

- generating
    
- processing
    
- storing
    
- containing
    
- extracting
    
- upgrading
    

it should belong to another mod.

This keeps Fistula as the dedicated transportation layer of Ars Mundi.

---

# Final Philosophy

Fistula represents the connections between systems.

Resources are generated.

Machines process them.

Energy powers them.

Storage preserves them.

But none of these systems can operate as a large infrastructure without movement.

Pipes carry fluids.

Tubes carry items.

Cables carry energy.

Conveyor belts carry physical objects.

Together, they turn isolated machines into a connected system.

**Fistula — The Arteries of Ars Mundi.**
