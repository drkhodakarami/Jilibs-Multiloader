# Fistula

**"Fistula"** (pronounced *FIS-too-la*) — Latin for "pipe, tube, conduit — the hollow passages through which everything flows."

Resources sit in one place. Machines need them in another. Fistula builds the arteries that connect your entire operation.

## What's Inside

**Item Transport Cables:**
- Move items between machines, storage, and processing systems
- Connect Machinum outputs to Cista inputs automatically
- Route items exactly where they need to go

**Energy Transport Cables:**
- Carry Potestas energy from generators to machines
- Efficient power distribution across your base

**Fluid Transport Pipes:**
- Move fluids from Tractus extraction to Cita storage
- Connect fluid-based systems together
- Handle water, lava, and other fluids in the ecosystem

**Network Logic:**
- Intelligent routing — items go where they're needed
- Expandable networks that grow with your base
- Connects the entire industrial ecosystem together

## How It Fits

Fistula depends on **Materia** (alloys: Resistant, Vibrant, Signalum, Energetic) and **Potestas**. It belongs to **Ars Mundi**. It's the connective tissue between extraction (Tractus), storage (Vas/Cista), processing (Machinum), and generation (Potestas).

## Why You Want It

Every industrial Minecraft base eventually needs transport. Fistula provides the pipes and cables that turn isolated machines into a connected ecosystem. Your base goes from rooms of machines to one living system.
