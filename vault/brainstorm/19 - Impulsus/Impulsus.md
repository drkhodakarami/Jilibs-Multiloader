# Impulsus - Mod Architecture Knowledge Base

## Overview

Impulsus is the redstone and signal-processing mod of the [Ars Mundi](https://chatgpt.com/c/Ars%20Mundi) ecosystem.

Name:

Impulsus

Pronunciation:

IM-pul-sus

Meaning:

Impulsus comes from Latin and refers to:

- impulse
    
- push
    
- driving force
    
- stimulation
    
- impetus
    
- sudden movement
    

The name represents the purpose of this mod:

> Creating, controlling, transforming, and responding to signals.

Impulsus expands the redstone systems of Minecraft with additional mechanisms for controlling and manipulating redstone signals.

It provides:

- redstone utilities
    
- signal generators
    
- logic systems
    
- timing mechanisms
    
- signal processing
    
- future redstone-based devices
    

Impulsus answers:

> How can the player control and manipulate the flow of redstone signals?

---

# Role in Ars Mundi

Ars Mundi is divided into different disciplines.

Each mod represents a specific area of mastery.

Impulsus represents:

> The discipline of signals, logic, timing, and control.

Minecraft already provides the foundation of redstone.

Impulsus builds upon that foundation.

It provides additional mechanisms that allow players to create more complex and controllable redstone systems.

---

# Core Philosophy

## Redstone Is A Language

Redstone is more than a power source.

It is a system of:

- signals
    
- conditions
    
- timing
    
- logic
    
- automation
    
- control
    

Players can already use vanilla redstone to construct complex machines.

Impulsus expands the vocabulary available to them.

The goal is not to replace vanilla redstone.

The goal is to provide additional building blocks for more advanced systems.

---

# Impulsus Is Not Machinum

A critical design separation exists between Impulsus and [Machinum](https://chatgpt.com/c/Machinum).

Machinum provides:

- machines
    
- processing
    
- automation
    
- industrial operations
    

Impulsus provides:

- signals
    
- logic
    
- timing
    
- redstone control
    

A machine may use a redstone signal.

The machine belongs to Machinum.

The mechanism generating or manipulating that signal belongs to Impulsus.

The distinction is:

> Machinum performs the operation.

> Impulsus controls when and how the operation happens.

---

# Impulsus Is Not Potestas

Another important separation exists between Impulsus and [Potestas](https://chatgpt.com/c/Potestas).

Potestas provides:

- energy generation
    
- energy storage
    
- energy infrastructure
    

Impulsus provides:

- redstone power
    
- redstone signals
    
- signal manipulation
    
- logic
    

Redstone energy and Ars Mundi energy are separate systems.

The distinction is:

> Potestas provides power for machines.

> Impulsus provides signals for control.

# Ticking Clock

One of the planned Impulsus systems is a ticking clock.

The ticking clock provides an automatic repeating redstone signal.

It can be used to:

- create repeating events
    
- automate periodic actions
    
- replace complicated clock circuits
    
- control machines
    
- trigger other redstone mechanisms
    

The player should be able to configure its timing according to the intended use.

The ticking clock represents one of the simplest forms of signal automation:

> Generate a signal repeatedly without requiring a manually constructed redstone clock.

---

# Logic Gates

Impulsus will provide logic gates for constructing logical redstone systems.

Possible gates include:

- AND
    
- OR
    
- NOT
    
- XOR
    
- NAND
    
- NOR
    
- XNOR
    

These gates allow players to construct logical conditions directly.

Instead of building complex combinations of:

- redstone torches
    
- repeaters
    
- comparators
    
- dust
    

the player can use dedicated logic components.

The goal is not to make redstone less interesting.

It is to make advanced logic more accessible and compact.

- separating signals

Impulsus can provide dedicated timing mechanisms for:

- delays
    
- intervals
    
- pulse durations
    
- repeating cycles

These systems should provide more deliberate control than manually constructing large redstone clocks.

Impulsus therefore treats:

- signal strength
    
- pulse duration
    
- delay

# Compact Logic

One of the major goals of Impulsus is to make complex redstone systems more compact.

A complicated circuit that occupies a large area may potentially be represented by a smaller dedicated component.

This does not remove complexity.

It moves complexity from:

> Physical wiring

to:

> Logical design.

The player still needs to understand what the system should do.

Impulsus simply provides better tools for expressing that design.

# Ars Mundi Relationship

In the complete Ars Mundi ecosystem, Impulsus provides the **control layer**.

Materia provides resources.

Machinum processes them.

Potestas provides energy.

Fistula transports materials and energy.

Cista stores them.

Tractus extracts fluids.

Impulsus controls the systems.

This creates a clear separation between:

> What a system does.

and:

> When and why it does it.

---

# Long-Term Philosophy

Impulsus should remain the dedicated home for redstone-related mechanics.

Whenever a new feature is proposed, the question should be:

> Is this fundamentally about creating, transforming, processing, or responding to a redstone signal?

If yes:

It may belong in Impulsus.

If it instead focuses on:

- generating energy
    
- processing materials
    
- transporting resources
    
- storing resources
    
- extracting fluids
    
- manipulating the world
    

it should belong to another mod.

This keeps Impulsus focused while allowing it to grow into a comprehensive redstone expansion.

# Final Philosophy

Impulsus represents the language of control.

Machines can perform work.

Generators can provide energy.

Pipes can move resources.

Storage can preserve them.

But something must tell all of these systems:

> When to act.

> When to stop.

> What condition to follow.

> What should happen next.

Redstone provides that language.

Impulsus expands its vocabulary.

**Impulsus — The Logic of the World.**
