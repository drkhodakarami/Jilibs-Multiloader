hese are the planned machines :
pulveriser :
will get raw ore, pulverize them to dust, 1 raw ore gives 2 dust

energetic furnace
equivalant of vanilla furance, just works on 4 simultaneous recipes instead of one and uses energy

cast publisher
prints casts that will be used in smeltery later as the guide for crafting

smeltery
the smeltery machine, accepts ingredients, getting the pattern from casts, creates other type of things

alloy mixer
will be used to mix different ingredients to create alloys

builder
builds any building from the printed schema

printer
prints a schema from a file. these prints will be used in builder later

pump
will pump fluids from the world into their internal tank or any tank that sits above them

chunk loader
will keep a chunk loaded

animal feeder
will feed animals automatically for breading

tesseract
will bridge between two blocks remotely, works between dimensions

single block farm
this will be used to provide food using a single block and it will be like having a 9 by 9 farm for that food

librarian cage
this single block will sit in the middle of a village trading hall and provide different types of exchanges with an easy to use organized interface

and many many more machines that will be planned or added in future.

the main thing is that, there will be no real "tech" mod with progression here. any machine will do a single special job that will help players and make their life easier
