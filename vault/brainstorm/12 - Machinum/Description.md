# Machinum

**"Machinum"** (pronounced *ma-KEE-num*) — Latin for "machine, mechanism, apparatus — the construction of systems that transform raw into refined."

Machinum is where Minecraft becomes an industrial powerhouse. Thirteen focused machines, each doing one job exceptionally well.

## What's Inside

**Processing Chain:**
- **Pulveriser** — Crush ores into dust. 2× yield from every ore vein.
- **Energetic Furnace** — Four parallel cooking slots. Cook everything four times faster.
- **Alloy Mixer** — Combine materials into Materia's functional alloys.
- **Smeltery + Cast Publisher** — Create casts and produce components at industrial scale.

**Construction Pipeline:**
- **Printer** → **Schema** → **Builder** — Design a structure, save it as a blueprint, build it automatically. Architecture at the press of a button.

**Utility Machines:**
- **Pump** — Fluid extraction at scale (energy-powered, works on fluid blocks in world)
- **Chunk Loader** — Keep your machines running even when you're far away
- **Animal Feeder** — Automated animal care
- **Single Block Farm** — Compact food production
- **Tesseract** — Cross-dimensional block binding
- **Librarian Cage** — Centralized trading — bring the villagers to you

**The Mold System:**
Cast Publisher creates molds. Smeltery reads them. Production methods that belong to Machinum, not Materia.

**Recipe Philosophy: "A Gear is a Gear"**
Same item as a crafting table — different production path. Machinum gives you scale, not different items.

## How It Fits

Machinum depends on **Materia** (materials, components) and **Potestas** (energy). It belongs to **Ars Mundi** only. It transforms raw resources into processed output at scale.

## Why You Want It

Manual crafting becomes  impossible. Thirteen machines that each solve one industrial problem. The moment your first Pulveriser doubles your ore output, you'll understand why Machinum exists.
